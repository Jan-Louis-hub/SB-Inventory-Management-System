Features to build
I'd divide the application into three stages so that you get a working system early rather than attempting every feature at once.

Stage 1 — Core inventory (MVP)
Build first
- Dashboard with total products, stock value and low-stock alerts.
- Add, edit, view and delete or archive products.
- Product categories, SKU codes and barcodes.
- Search, filtering, sorting and pagination.
- Stock-in and stock-out adjustments.
- Low-stock and out-of-stock indicators.




Stage 2 — Business operations
- Supplier management.
- Purchase orders and receiving stock.
- Sales recording and automatic stock deduction.
- Customer records, if needed.
- Stock movement history and audit trail.
- Profit estimates, sales reports and CSV export.



Stage 3 — Advanced features
- Login, roles and permissions.
- Barcode scanning.
- PDF invoices and reports.
- Expiry-date and batch tracking, where relevant.
